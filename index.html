Solo Trading Business Operating Model

Zero-Employee Business Architecture & Automation Playbook

This operational framework outlines how a solo trader can manage capital, strategy execution, risk management, and regulatory tax compliance without requiring administrative staff, marketing, or a Chartered Accountant for routine filings.

1. Core Business Pillar Stack

1. Strategy & Backtesting
Engine: Streak (Zerodha)
Automates technical strategy logic, entry/exit indicators, and dynamic Stop Loss / Trailing Stop Loss triggers.
2. Options Analytics
Engine: Sensibull
Calculates real-time Greeks, Open Interest (OI) analysis, multi-leg payoff diagrams, and delta-neutral hedging setups.
3. Fundamental Research
Engine: Tijori Finance
Provides granular sector exposure, balance sheet deep-dives, supply chain mapping, and operational metrics.
4. Automated Tax & Compliance
Engine: Quicko (API Integration)
Direct broker-to-tax sync fetching trade books, salary AIS data, and auto-populating ITR-3 without Tally.

3. Regulatory Provisions & Audit Rules

Section 44AB (Tax Audit Rule)
Because 100% of Zerodha transactions take place digitally via banking channels, the mandatory tax audit threshold is ₹10 Crore Turnover. No CA audit is legally required below this level.
Section 87A (New Tax Regime)
Total taxable income (Salary + Net Trading Profits) up to ₹12,00,000 receives a full rebate under the New Tax Regime, resulting in ₹0 net tax payable.

4. Annual 15-Minute Weekend Workflow

Phase Action Item Time Required
Step 1 Log in to Quicko using Zerodha Kite credentials (Auto-syncs trades & MF). 3 Minutes
Step 2 Connect ITD Account via Aadhaar OTP (Auto-fetches Form 16 & AIS data). 3 Minutes
Step 3 Upload CAMS / KFintech statement for non-Zerodha HDFC Mutual Funds (if any). 2 Minutes
Step 4 Input 31st March closing bank balance & Zerodha ledger balance ("No Accounts Case"). 2 Minutes
Step 5 Select New Tax Regime, review ₹0 tax computation, and e-file ITR-3 via Aadhaar OTP. 3 Minutes

5. Executive Summary: The Chief Risk Officer Role

With all administrative, sales, accounting, and tax filing tasks fully automated through technology, your sole ongoing operational responsibility is serving as the Chief Risk Officer—monitoring strategy drift, managing total portfolio drawdowns, and approving execution notifications.
